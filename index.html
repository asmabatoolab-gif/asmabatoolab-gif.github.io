About Me

Bridging rigorous geopolitical inquiry with evidence-based policy and grassroots social action.

I am an International Relations researcher and development practitioner based in Islamabad, Pakistan. My academic foundation is anchored in an MPhil in International Relations from the National Defence University (NDU), Islamabad, completed in November 2021, following a Bachelor's degree in Defence and Diplomatic Studies from Fatima Jinnah Women University (FJWU) in 2017.

My professional background spans rigorous academic research, higher education teaching, non-governmental organisation (NGO) programme implementation, administration, and inter-agency stakeholder coordination. As a former Lecturer at Bahria University Islamabad, I designed and delivered undergraduate courses in international relations and political affairs, mentoring students in qualitative methodology and critical analysis.

In the humanitarian and development spheres, my work with organisations including E HANDS, HelpAge, and Muslim Aid has encompassed programme coordination, stakeholder liaison, event management, and field monitoring. I am deeply committed to research, policy engagement, and practical social impact—focusing on foreign policy, international relations, religion-based racism, social justice, child protection, and community development.
